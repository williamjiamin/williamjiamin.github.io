SimpleMath Support

Getting started

Open SimpleMath, choose a language and a world, then begin with the next available lesson. Progress is stored on the device.

Common questions

Can I change the language?
Yes. Open Settings and choose Chinese or English.

How do I reset progress?
Delete SimpleMath from the device, then install it again. This removes the app's locally stored learning data.

Does SimpleMath need an account or internet connection?
No. The app does not require an account and its learning features work offline.
