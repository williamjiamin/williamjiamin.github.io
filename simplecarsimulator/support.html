HomePrivacy PolicySupport

Need help? Email williamjiaminus@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Restore purchases

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Remove Ads and the Garage Pass are restored; coin packs are one-time consumables and are not restorable.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

The free version shows a menu banner and optional rewarded videos. Buying Remove Ads or the Garage Pass removes all ads permanently. You can change ad-consent choices in Settings → Privacy choices.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress unless it is part of your device backup.

Game is running slowly or hot

Open Settings and lower the graphics quality, then restart the app.
