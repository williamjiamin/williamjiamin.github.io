Simple Car Simulator ("the app") is published by WANG LIPAI ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: the game itself does not ask for your name, email or any account, and your progress stays on your device. Advertising in the free version is provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, cars, paint designs, custom tracks, settings and purchase records are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too (unless it is included in your own device backup).

Microphone (optional)

The optional Sound Booth feature can record your voice to create custom in-game sounds. The app asks for permission first. Recordings are processed and stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad on menu screens and offers optional rewarded videos (for example, to double race coins). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Coarse location (derived from IP address)Advertising, fraud prevention
Device identifiers (app-scoped identifiers; the app does not request the Advertising Identifier/IDFA and does not use App Tracking Transparency)Advertising, analytics, frequency capping

This data is not linked to your identity by us and is not used by us to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent: where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy choices").

Remove Ads: purchasing Remove Ads or the Garage Pass stops all ads permanently. Optional rewarded bonuses are then granted without showing an ad.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app receives only Apple-signed confirmation of which products you own, which is stored on your device so purchases work offline and can be restored.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising and App Store processing described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

Data retention and your choices

Data saved by the app stays on your device until you delete it or the app. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above; you can also reset or limit ad personalization in your device's privacy settings, or buy Remove Ads to stop ad requests entirely.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
