Privacy Policy

WiFi AR (iOS) — last updated 15 September 2026

WiFi AR does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the app measures stays on your device.

What the app does on your device

  • Camera. Used only to render the augmented-reality view that places signal readings in the space around you. Camera frames are processed live on device and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Bluetooth. Used only to discover nearby Bluetooth devices and read their signal strength so you can locate them. Discovered device names and signal readings stay on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Nearby Interaction (Ultra Wideband). Used only, on supported hardware and with compatible accessories, to show direction and distance to an accessory you are actively looking for.
  • LiDAR room capture. On supported iPhones, RoomPlan builds an outline of walls, doors and windows on device. The outline is stored only in the app's own storage and is never uploaded.
  • Floor plans you import. A photo or PDF you choose is copied into the app's storage on your device so the coverage map can be drawn on it. The app only receives the single file you pick; it has no access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Apple Intelligence summary. On iOS 26 devices that support it, a short summary of your survey is generated by Apple's on-device model. Your readings are not sent to any server for this.
  • Saved scans and reports. Signal maps, rooms, plans and any PDF or CSV report you export are written to your device's app storage. Reports leave your device only when you choose to share them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

To measure connection quality and to run the built-in speed test, the app sends ordinary network requests to public test endpoints and measures how long they take. These requests carry no personal information, no identifiers, and no data about you — only the request itself. We do not operate these endpoints and we do not receive their logs.

Data we collect

None. In App Store terms, this app's privacy label is Data Not Collected. Because no data is collected, none is shared, sold, or used to track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the unlock has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Children

The app is rated for all ages, contains no user-generated content, no messaging, and no advertising, and collects no data from anyone — including children.
