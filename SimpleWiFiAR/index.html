Frequently asked

Why doesn't it show Wi-Fi signal strength in dBm?
iOS does not let third-party apps read Wi-Fi radio signal strength — that API is reserved by Apple. So the Wi-Fi readings in this app measure real connection quality (latency, jitter, and packet loss to the network) rather than raw radio strength. Bluetooth is different: those readings are true device signal strength (RSSI).
The AR view is blank or won't start.
AR needs camera permission and a reasonably lit, textured space to lock onto — blank walls and dark rooms are hard to track. Check Settings › WiFi AR › Camera, then move the phone slowly around the room.
No Bluetooth devices appear.
Check Settings › WiFi AR › Bluetooth is allowed, and that Bluetooth is on in Control Center. Only devices that are actively advertising are discoverable — headphones already connected to another phone often won't show up until they're put back into pairing mode.
How do I align an imported floor plan?
Open Coverage › Map, tap Set position, stand somewhere easy to find and tap that spot on the plan, then walk a few metres to a second spot and tap it too. Scan must be running so the phone knows where it is. After that, readings land on the plan and rooms get scored automatically.
Where is the LiDAR room scan?
The Scan room button appears on the Map only on iPhones with a LiDAR scanner (Pro models). On other iPhones, import a floor plan or draw rooms on the map.
What does Pro include?
Pro is a single one-time purchase, never a subscription. It unlocks PDF and CSV survey reports, floor-plan import, unlimited saved scans, three premium AR themes, and speed-test history. Everything else is free.
I bought Pro and it's not unlocked.
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
How do I delete my data?
Settings › Clear All Data inside the app removes every reading, and Reset rooms & floor plan removes rooms and any imported plan. Deleting the app removes everything.
